Claudini: Autoresearch Discovers State-of-the-Art Adversarial Attack Algorithms for LLMs

LLM agents like Claude Code can not only write code but also be used for autonomous AI research and engineering . We show that an autoresearch-style pipeline powered by Claude Code discovers novel white-box adversarial attack algorithms that significantly outperform all existing (30+) methods in jailbreaking and prompt injection evaluations. Extending the findings of~ , our results are an early demonstration that incremental safety and security research can be automated using LLM agents. White-box adversarial red-teaming is particularly well-suited for this: existing methods provide strong starting points, and the optimization objective yields dense, quantitative feedback. We release all discovered attacks alongside baseline implementations and evaluation code at https://github.com/romovpa/claudini.

Multi-Agent Reasoning with Consistency Verification Improves Uncertainty Calibration in Medical MCQA

arXiv:2603.24481 多智能体一致性验证提升医学多选题不确定性校准 John Ray B. Martinez et al.
📄 论文摘要
Miscalibrated confidence scores are a practical obstacle to deploying AI in clinical settings. A model that is always overconfident offers no useful signal for deferral. We present a multi-agent framework that combines domain-specific specialist agents with Two-Phase Verification and S-Score Weighted Fusion to improve both calibration and discrimination in medical multiple-choice question answering. Four specialist agents (respiratory, cardiology, neurology, gastroenterology) generate independent diagnoses using Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct. Each diagnosis is then subjected to a two-phase self-verification process that measures internal consistency and produces a Specialist Confidence Score (S-score). The S-scores drive a weighted fusion strategy that selects the final answer and calibrates the reported confidence. We evaluate across four experimental settings, covering 100-question and 250-question high-disagreement subsets of both MedQA-USMLE and MedMCQA. Calibration improvement is the central finding, with ECE reduced by 49-74% across all four settings, including the harder MedMCQA benchmark where these gains persist even when absolute accuracy is constrained by knowledge-intensive recall demands. On MedQA-250, the full system achieves ECE = 0.091 (74.4% reduction over the single-specialist baseline) and AUROC = 0.630 (+0.056) at 59.2% accuracy. Ablation analysis identifies Two-Phase Verification as the primary calibration driver and multi-agent reasoning as the primary accuracy driver. These results establish that consistency-based verification produces more reliable uncertainty estimates across diverse medical question types, providing a practical confidence signal for deferral in safety-critical clinical AI applications.

ClawKeeper: Comprehensive Safety Protection for OpenClaw Agents Through Skills, Plugins, and Watchers

arXiv:2603.24414 ClawKeeper：基于技能、插件与监控器的OpenClaw智能体安全防护 Songyang Liu et al.
📄 论文摘要
OpenClaw作为领先的开源自主体运行时，其强大的工具集成、本地文件访问和Shell命令执行能力带来了严重的安全漏洞，如敏感数据泄露、权限提升和恶意第三方技能执行。现有安全措施高度碎片化，无法提供全面保护。为此，本文提出了ClawKeeper，一个实时安全框架，通过三个互补的架构层集成多维保护机制：基于技能的保护在指令级别操作，将结构化安全策略注入代理上下文；基于插件的保护作为内部运行时强制执行器，提供配置强化、主动威胁检测和持续行为监控；基于观察者的保护引入了一种解耦的系统级安全中间件，持续验证代理状态演化，支持实时执行干预。评估表明ClawKeeper在不同威胁场景下具有有效性和鲁棒性。
🔧 关键技术
  • 基于技能的保护（Skill-based protection）：在指令级别注入结构化安全策略，强制执行环境特定约束和跨平台边界
  • 基于插件的保护（Plugin-based protection）：作为内部运行时强制执行器，提供配置强化、主动威胁检测和持续行为监控
  • 基于观察者的保护（Watcher-based protection）：解耦的系统级安全中间件，持续验证代理状态演化，支持实时执行干预（如停止高风险操作或强制人工确认）
💡 核心创新
  • 提出了ClawKeeper框架，首次为OpenClaw代理提供跨技能、插件和观察者三个架构层的全面实时安全保护
  • 引入了基于观察者（Watcher）的新型解耦系统级安全中间件范式，能够在不耦合代理内部逻辑的情况下实现实时执行干预
  • 实现了多维度的保护机制集成，覆盖从指令级别、运行时到系统级的完整代理生命周期，解决了现有安全措施碎片化的问题
📊 关键结论
  • ClawKeeper框架通过技能、插件和观察者三个互补层的集成，有效解决了OpenClaw代理面临的安全漏洞问题
  • 基于观察者的保护范式具有作为下一代自主代理系统安全基础构建块的强大潜力
  • 广泛的定性和定量评估表明，ClawKeeper在不同威胁场景下表现出有效性和鲁棒性
